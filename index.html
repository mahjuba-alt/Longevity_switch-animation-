Scene A — The molecular world

EstablishedCells sense growth signals through receptors in their membrane.

EstablishedIGF1R is a receptor tyrosine kinase. IGF ligands bind its extracellular α subunits.

EstablishedBinding activates the kinase domains and relays the signal through IRS, PI3K–AKT and FOXO. Schematic view.

Scene B — The worm research inspiration

EstablishedC. elegans carries daf-2, which encodes an insulin/IGF-1-like receptor.

EstablishedReducing daf-2 signaling extends lifespan in worms, in a way that depends on the FOXO factor DAF-16.

Findings in worms do not automatically establish the same effect in humans.
